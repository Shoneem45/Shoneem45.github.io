❌ The Real-Life Problem (Current Hospitals)

Chaotic Outpatient (OPD) Overcrowding

  • • First-Come-First-Serve bottleneck: Critical patients wait 4+ hours in the same queue as mild cold/cough patients.
  • • Revolving Door trap: 15% of chronic patients repeatedly return to the hospital, consuming over 50% of doctors' time.
  • • Doctor Burnout: Physicians only get 2–3 minutes per consultation, leading to diagnostic errors.
✅ The Data Analytics Solution (Our Model)

Data-Driven Triage & Home-Care Diversion

  • • Automated Priority Queue: Severe cases (Code Red) are fast-tracked to doctors within 15 minutes.
  • • Chronic Home-Care Pathway: Chronic patients are assigned remote tele-monitoring, reducing physical visits by 35%.
  • • Clinical Capacity Unlocked: Frees 500+ doctor hours, allowing physicians 15–20 minutes with high-risk patients.
